# Author-result reproduction: miRNA and ozone

The deposited miR-574-5p signal is lower at both PM doses. At 5 mg/kg, the mean difference is -0.2694 source signal units, with nominal LSD p = 0.0368 and primary limma p = 0.0253, but BH q = 0.312. The direction and nominal pairwise evidence agree with the array claim; omnibus ANOVA p = 0.0788 fails a protected LSD gate. The paper does not specify that gate or its array-specific selection and multiplicity settings. These arrays do not reproduce the separate qPCR, BACE1, ChIP, rescue or behavioral experiments.

The ozone source genotype CSV selection is exactly reconstructed: 1,490 higher and 1,011 lower genes at nominal p < 0.05 and |log2FC| > 0.3; these sets equal supplementary S2. Applying candidate DESeq2 models to the same 24 libraries quantified with Salmon gives partial concordance. The genotype-only candidate has 15,559 common finite effects and Spearman ρ = 0.865; it selects 2,362 genes versus 2,501 published genes. Within the jointly testable exact-symbol universe, 1,527 selected genes overlap (Jaccard = 0.612).

For E4/E3 among 17-month O3 mice, 858 of 959 published S3 genes have finite exact-symbol estimates, with ρ = 0.962 and 98.8% direction agreement. Of the matched genes, 543 retain the candidate nominal criterion and 71 retain BH FDR < 0.05. The candidate's full subgroup list contains 1,136 genes. This selected-list comparison is conditional on the authors' selection and uses the same animals, rather than independent biological replication.

All four hemoglobin genes retain negative E4/E3 effects and BH significance. Several other highlighted targets retain their direction without equivalent nominal or FDR evidence. Bulk composition, vascular material and expression regulation cannot be distinguished here; BBB function is not inferred. Original HISAT2/featureCounts, the exact design and filter, annotation release and historical DESeq2 version are not fully reproduced. Candidate pooled models use DESeq2's default count-replacement setting, with affected-gene counts recorded and no animals removed. The new UpSet panels compare component-list membership on one shared universe; they are not interaction tests.
