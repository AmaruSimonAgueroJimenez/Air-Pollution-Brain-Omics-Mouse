# Measured sections and anatomical detection

Anterior cingulate cortex: all 14,582 registered measured cells from 18 original physical sections, with 10 deposited anatomical strata. Ten annotated genes retain exact raw counts and original coordinates. Median estimated slice AP separation is 182.7 µm within this regional subset.

Baseline regional detection: Fosb 1,418/14,582 (9.72%); Egr3 5,699/14,582 (39.08%); S1pr1 6,761/14,582 (46.37%). These fractions describe measured baseline detection, not the magnitude or location of a PM2.5 response.

Hypothalamus: all 37,811 registered measured cells from 20 original physical sections, with 49 deposited anatomical strata. Ten annotated genes retain exact raw counts and original coordinates. Median estimated slice AP separation is 182.5 µm within this regional subset.

Baseline regional detection: Tll1 667/37,811 (1.76%); Fosl2 2,461/37,811 (6.51%); Nr4a3 1,079/37,811 (2.85%). These fractions describe measured baseline detection, not the magnitude or location of a PM2.5 response.

Detection is the number of cells with raw count >0 divided by all measured cells in the displayed anatomical group, slice or occupied 100×100 µm slice bin, including measured zeros. Absent bins have no measurements and are not filled with zero. The single donor supplies descriptive proportions; cells are not independent biological replicates. Section maps use deposited experimental x/y, distinct from nonlinear CCF registration. There is no interpolation, relocation, smoothing or inferred PM2.5 effect.
