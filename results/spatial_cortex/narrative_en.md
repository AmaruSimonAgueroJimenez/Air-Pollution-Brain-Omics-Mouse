# Anterior cingulate cortex: measured reference and regional scenarios

The anterior cingulate cortex (ACA) extension retains **14,582 registered cells** from the unexposed male Zhuang-ABCA-2 donor: 7,784 ACAd and 6,798 ACAv cells, spanning 16 atlas classes and ten layer-level substructures. Source anatomical labels are preserved; there is no cell subsampling, mirrored hemisphere, or imputed expression. The ACA boundary is an anatomical proxy for the reported bulk dissection, whose exact surgical boundaries are not deposited.

All **1,122 physically measured MERFISH panel genes** remain selectable. Exact Ensembl gene-ID matching finds 960 genes in the primary cortical table, with 916 eligible finite estimates and **76 primary BH FDR<0.05 hits**. Every one of those 76 has at least one positive measured ACA count. Whole-transcriptome cortical leaders Hspa5, Pdia4 and Xbp1 are absent from this panel and must be interpreted from bulk measurements; they are not inserted into the spatial atlas.

The ranked spatial shortlist uses the lowest unrounded primary BH q values among measured eligible genes, with Ensembl-ID tie breaking. **Fosb** leads (primary q=2.2651×10⁻¹⁹; shrunken log2FC=0.9971), followed by Egr3 and S1pr1. Fosb is positive in **1,418 of 14,582 reference cells**. Its central regional multiplier is **1.996×**. The extended gallery shows those three genes on identical cell positions, with a common reference/scenario color scale within each gene. Their observed detection patterns differ, but the modeled multiplier is uniform across the entire selected ACA population.

The exposure evidence comes from **four PBS and four PM2.5 bulk animals**, using the primary eight-animal model, not the original-author 20-animal reproduction. The reference donor is a separate animal and does not reproduce the humanized α-synuclein experimental model. The transfer therefore provides a labeled hypothesis visualization, without identifying responding cells or correcting unresolved source sex/composition questions. Group-level MLE±1.96SE scenarios are not cellular confidence intervals.

Registration checks compare all 14,582 source cell labels with the 25µm anatomical volume: agreement is 93.54%. Imperfect point-to-voxel agreement is disclosed; no cells are moved or relabeled to improve it. All 1,122 gene payloads decode exactly to their extracted measured count vectors. The anatomical context uses 0.15mm whole-brain and 0.05mm ACA surface sampling; it does not alter cell expression.
