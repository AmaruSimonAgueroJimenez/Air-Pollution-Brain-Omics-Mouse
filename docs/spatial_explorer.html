Introduction

The three-dimensional cell cloud contains actual registered atlas coordinates. This extension makes its anatomical organization easier to inspect through original physical sections, cortical layers or hypothalamic nuclei, and maps of the fraction of measured cells in which a gene is detected. It does not rearrange cells or generate additional observations. The reference is the unexposed adult male Zhuang-ABCA-2, separate from the pollution experiments. (Zhang et al. 2023)

Methods

Cell identifiers join the original section metadata to the existing regional reference without changing cell order or counts. All registered regional cells are retained. Ten fixed measured candidates per region reproduce the prior gene-priority lists; this extension does not reselect exposure discoveries using the spatial maps.

Detection means an original imaging count greater than zero. For each displayed anatomical group or occupied spatial bin, the numerator is the number of positive-count cells and the denominator is every selected measured cell, including zeros. Filters change both numerator and denominator. Empty bins have no denominator and remain missing; they are not painted as zero detection. This is a detection fraction among sampled cells, not cells per tissue volume, biological absence, or the fraction of cells responding to PM2.5.

The same gene can be displayed as measured expression, binary detection, cell class or anatomy. Zeros are visible in a neutral color. Bin size and the number of supporting cells are explicit. Raw cell values and tables remain downloadable. A visually smooth map is not evidence of greater acquisition resolution: this donor was sampled at nominal 200 µm coronal intervals. (Zhang et al. 2023)

Interpretation and the Bayesian extension

Anatomical restrictions make the existing organization visible without moving a cell. The Bayesian tab estimates baseline expected log-expression at the existing cell locations and displays uncertainty separately. It does not turn a nondetection into an observed molecule. Its held-out prediction results, modeling assumptions and limitations are presented in the Bayesian chapter.

The experimental PM2.5 comparison remains the original regional bulk analysis and uniform scenario in the separate 3D viewers. These section maps do not identify exposed single cells or cell-type-specific responses.
