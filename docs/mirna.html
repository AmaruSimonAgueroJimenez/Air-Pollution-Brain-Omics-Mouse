Hippocampal miRNA: two PM doses

Analysis date

19 October 2026

Study question

GSE93967 measures hippocampal miRNA signals after saline or PM2.5 administered by oropharyngeal aspiration at 1 or 5 mg/kg every other day for four weeks. The deposited microarray experiment contains nine biological samples: three per group. The original report focused on miR-574-5p and additional functional experiments; this report reanalyzes the deposited array matrix and does not reproduce those additional experiments. (Ku et al. 2017)

Materials and methods

Input, annotation, and measurement scale

The GEO series matrix contains 1,268 feature rows. The GPL17912 platform table maps biological mouse miRNA probes to the supplied miRNA_ID labels. Historical platform identifiers are retained without a silent miRBase update or aggregation. Twenty-one control or unannotated rows are preserved in the source export but excluded from differential analysis. Two biological miRNA rows, mmu-miR-3963 and mmu-miR-6366, have zero variance across the nine samples and are also excluded.

GEO describes quantile normalization with GeneSpring but does not explicitly establish the log2 measurement scale of the deposited matrix. The supplied values include negative values and a large lower-bound pile-up. The analysis applies neither a second log transform nor a count-data model. Effects are labeled mean differences in published normalized signal units, with positive values indicating higher signal in the PM group. They are not interpreted as log2 fold changes or converted to fold changes.

Statistical model and detection flags

Limma fits a three-group linear model with one group-mean coefficient for saline, 1 mg/kg PM, and 5 mg/kg PM. The two planned contrasts compare each PM group with saline. Empirical Bayes moderation uses trend=True and robust=False; the optional omnibus test is a two-coefficient moderated F test for the two PM-minus-saline contrasts. (Ritchie et al. 2015)

Benjamini–Hochberg adjustment is applied across the retained miRNA rows within each pairwise contrast, and separately within the omnibus test. These adjustments do not provide a single pooled FDR guarantee across the three test families. Exported standard errors and pointwise 95% moderated t intervals refer to the reported signal differences; the intervals are not simultaneous or multiplicity-adjusted. (Benjamini and Hochberg 1995)

The original Agilent detection calls are retained. A low-detection flag identifies rows with fewer than three Present calls in every group. These rows remain in the primary testing family, with their flag available for interpretation. No biological sample is removed on the basis of PCA. PCA uses the 500 most variable retained features, centered without variance scaling, on the deposited signal scale.

Results

Quality and signal limitations

1,245 biological miRNA features enter the model. Of these, 823 receive the low-detection flag. Across the retained features and nine samples, 7,722 negative signal values are retained. The sample-specific floor near −3.3 dominates the lower portion of the signal distribution and limits the information available for many features.

PC1 and PC2 explain 38.6% and 18.7% of the variance in the PCA input. The three exposure groups do not separate cleanly in this display. A lack of clear PCA separation does not establish equivalence, and the nine samples remain in all models.

Relation to the original miR-574-5p report

The original publication investigated miR-574-5p with array discovery and additional biological experiments. The current secondary array analysis does not identify miR-574-5p at FDR < 0.05 in either prespecified pairwise contrast. This difference should not be described as replication of the original central mechanism, nor as refutation of experiments that were not reanalyzed here. (Ku et al. 2017)

Interpretation and limits

The small group sizes, extensive low-detection features, normalization-scale ambiguity, and within-group heterogeneity limit mechanistic interpretation. A positive array coefficient indicates a signal difference on the deposited scale. It does not establish the direction or magnitude of an unmeasured mRNA target, a validated miRNA–target interaction, or a change in a specific hippocampal cell population. For that reason, this analysis does not apply mRNA Gene Ontology enrichment directly to miRNA names.

#| label: mirna-original-candidate
candidate_rows = []
