Several relevant cortical genes

The cortex now has its own anatomical reference and its own exposure-effect estimates. Fosb, Egr3, S1pr1, Fosl2, Col19a1, Pcdh8, Iqgap2, Usp43, and Rorb, together with the noncoding C730002L08Rik, form the ten strongest eligible measured priorities. The viewer provides gene shortcuts, what each curated gene encodes, its molecular function and official source links. A comparison table can retain up to three measured genes at once.

The default Fosb central regional multiplier is 1.996×. It has a positive measured count in 1,418 of 14,582 reference cells. Detection describes this atlas donor; it does not identify exposure-responsive cells.

Measured reference beside a simulated regional scenario. Both scenes preserve the same cell positions and anatomy. The regional multiplier comes from GSE297423’s primary eight-animal model, not from exposed atlas cells. The current atlas donor is not a matched control for the humanized α-synuclein mice.

What the genes encode and how to compare them

The gene profiles retain the strongest bulk genes even if they are absent from MERFISH. In particular, Hspa5, Pdia4 and Xbp1 remain priorities in the bulk analysis; their spatial expression is not invented. Noncoding genes are identified as RNA products rather than assigned a protein.

Select a gene shortcut, inspect its product/function card, and add it to the three-gene comparison. Detection percentage and mean imaging count include all measured cells, including zeros. Those counts are separate from RNA-seq normalized counts and from regional fold changes. Different probe efficiencies and biological distributions limit direct comparisons of count levels between genes.

A positive bulk estimate means higher regional RNA in PM than PBS under that model. It does not establish higher protein abundance, a responding cell type, or a causal role. The primary results retain sample-quality concerns documented in the reproduction audit.

Anatomical and statistical scope

The mask is ACAd and ACAv with all their layers, restricted to the registered anterior cingulate cortex. It contains 7,784 dorsal and 6,798 ventral ACA cells, with no cell subsampling or mirrored hemisphere. It does not represent every cortical area. This is an anatomical ACA proxy for the reported dissection; exact surgical boundaries were not deposited.

Exact Ensembl identifiers match 960 of the 1,122 measured panel genes to primary cortex effects. Of these, 916 meet the viewer’s estimability rule; 76 have primary BH q < 0.05. Missing or unreliable effects remain reference-only. The central scene uses the saved shrunken coefficient, while alternative interval scenarios use MLE ± 1.96 SE at group level.
