Quality, normalization, and sample relationships

PC1 explains 76.2% of variance and PC2 17.7%. The dominant variation does not form a clean exposure-group separation: FA1 and FA2 are distinct, whereas FA3 and FA4 lie closer to the PM samples. This is observed heterogeneity, not evidence of a known batch covariate. No principal component was used to manufacture a batch correction.

Median-of-ratios size factors were estimated within the seven-sample primary dataset. Size factors, count distributions, and Cook’s diagnostics are available in the source tables. The source does not provide the per-sample RIN or batch information needed to resolve the observed heterogeneity.

The analysis retained 19,611 of 50,417 genes. 19,452 genes received a finite adjusted p-value; 159 were filtered from inferential reporting by Cook’s diagnostics. At FDR < 0.05, 11 genes differ between PM and FA: 2 higher and 9 lower in PM. 4 also have absolute shrunken log₂FC ≥ 1.

All eleven primary hits retain their direction in seven of seven fits, but none remains FDR-significant in all seven fits. The total discovery count ranges from zero to 825 across omissions. This sensitivity limits the certainty of gene-level discovery and any spatial scenario derived from it. Omitting a control that increases the discovery count is not a justification for excluding that control.

The independent R/DESeq2 cross-check uses the same 19,452 finite-p hypotheses and the same 159 Cook-filtered genes. All eleven primary hits remain significant in R; R additionally identifies Tnrc6c, whose FDR is 0.0399 in R and 0.0521 in Python. Tll1 remains significant in both implementations (FDR 0.00330 in R; 0.00350 in Python). Effect estimates agree closely (Pearson correlation >0.999999). This software comparison does not provide biological replication or resolve sample heterogeneity. The fixed Python primary result is retained, including its threshold-sensitive genes.

Functional enrichment

GO Biological Process over-representation analysis found 0 higher-expression and 0 lower-expression significant terms after BH correction. The small, partially annotated discovery list does not support a confirmed pathway conclusion.

Preranked GSEA detects broader signed rank patterns, including overlapping ciliary and neuronal/synaptic annotations, but these results are exploratory. They do not remove the control heterogeneity, establish independent pathway mechanisms, or substitute for additional animals. The functional-enrichment page reports the full tested universe, annotation coverage, rank exclusions, and algorithmic FDR separately from ORA.

Spatial interpretation

Tll1 is the only primary FDR hit measured in this atlas panel. Its bulk MLE log₂FC is -1.344, its shrunken log₂FC is -1.224, and its FDR is 3.50e-03. The central visualization multiplier is therefore 0.428×; the pointwise group MLE interval corresponds to 0.273–0.569×. These multipliers define scenarios on the measured reference; they are not per-cell exposure measurements.
